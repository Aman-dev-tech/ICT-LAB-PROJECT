APPLIED PHYSICS SYLLABUS

Reflection

Behavior of light when it strikes a reflective surface.

Refraction

Bending of light as it passes through different media.

Diffraction

Spreading of light waves through narrow openings.

Interference

Combination of light waves to form patterns.

Polarization

Orientation of light waves in a single plane.

Electrostatics

Study of electric charges at rest.

Electrodynamics

Electric charges in motion and electric currents.

Magnetism

Magnetic fields and magnetic forces.

Electromagnetism

Relationship between electricity and magnetism.
